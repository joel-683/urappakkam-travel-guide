3

BUY A TICKET · CHECK BEFORE BOARDING

Take a train to Urappakkam

  • Ask for a suburban train ticket to Urappakkam (UPM).
  • Ask staff: “Which train stops at Urappakkam?”
  • Check the station display and announcements for the platform and destination.
  • Board only after confirming the train stops at Urappakkam.

Do not rely on an old timetable or a fixed platform number. Services and platforms can change.

4

YOU’RE ON YOUR WAY

Stay on until Urappakkam

Do not get off at Tambaram. Keep listening to announcements and checking station names. Urappakkam is beyond Perungalathur and Vandalur on this route, but confirm your train's stops.

If something goes wrong

  • Move to a safe, staffed place and ask railway staff for help.
  • Show them the destination name: Urappakkam (UPM).
  • For railway assistance, ask staff for the appropriate official contact. Indian Railways enquiry number 139 may help with railway information.
  • Do not follow a stranger away from the station or share your phone unlock code.
